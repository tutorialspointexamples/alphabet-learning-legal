Privacy Policy — Kids Learning

Last updated: October 12, 2026
App: Kids Learning (com.w3stech.kidslearning)
Contact: support@w3stech.com

Kids Learning is made by W3S Tech. This policy explains how the app handles information. See also the Terms of Use.

1. Who this app is for

Kids Learning is an educational app for young children, intended to be used with a parent or caregiver.

2. Information we store

Progress data (such as stars, coins, letter progress, and practice streaks) is saved on this device only. The app does not require an account or login to play.

3. What we do not collect

We do not ask for a child's name, email, phone number, or location to use the learning activities. We do not sell personal information.

4. Speech and audio

Spoken feedback uses audio clips bundled with the app. On some Android devices, the system text-to-speech feature may be used for phrases that do not have a bundled clip. That processing happens on the device.

5. Parent area

The Parent Area is protected by a PIN chosen on this device (default PIN is 1234 until changed). Keep this PIN private so children cannot open parent tools.

6. Third parties

This version of Kids Learning does not include advertising SDKs or social login. Your device maker and app store may collect standard device or install analytics under their own policies.

7. Data retention and deletion

Progress stays on the device until you clear the app data or uninstall Kids Learning.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@w3stech.com

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest update.
